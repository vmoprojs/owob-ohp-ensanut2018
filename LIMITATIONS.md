# Scientific and reproducibility limits

The analytical pivot is selected. The 23,621 F4→19,750 selection mechanism is not identified; 3,871 are outside it. Reconstructing survey variance does not repair an unknown selection mechanism. Weighted values describe the selected analytical domain under the F4 assumptions and are not certified national prevalence estimates.

OHRP6 is reported experience, not clinical disease diagnosis. Cross-sectional odds ratios do not establish causation. The overall adjusted obesity contrast is borderline; the children's global nutrition test is not conventionally significant. Nutrition-by-sex p=0.314558 does not demonstrate effect modification. Avoid subgroup p-value comparisons.

S2 uses independently verified exact measurement-age calculations and official F1 readings. oedema=n is operationally assumed, not clinically verified. Stored SAV Z/classifications have uncertain historical provenance; numerical compatibility does not establish historical origin. Original SAV categories are retained as sensitivity only. There are 1,201 aggregate category differences (6.081% of 19,750); the 113 historical boundary discrepancies and later S2 differences are distinct issues.

OWOBNEW_SES_MCA_v1 is a newly constructed material-asset index. Full replay requires the nonredistributed pivot and certified adjudication/model contracts in addition to official public files. Public reproduction validates frozen aggregates and their rendering; it cannot independently validate participant selection or generate new individual estimates.
